## Exact corresponding source

The exact upstream Stockfish.js commit matching the engine files distributed by NexoChess is:

https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/fe65b8845c65fd389a56887e004fa0a3436159e2

That tree contains the engine source, build system, generated wrappers and WebAssembly files used for this release.
